## 1. Introduction

KAI Token is an ERC-20 digital asset deployed on the Base blockchain.

The project is designed around transparency, digital identity, decentralized participation and the gradual development of a community-driven ecosystem.

KAI is built with a long-term approach. The project prioritizes clear public information, verifiable blockchain data and responsible ecosystem growth.
